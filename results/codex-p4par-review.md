# Independent review of p4par

Reviewed `CHARTER.md`, `ref/p4/p4par.c`, and the repetition-only diff of `p4.c` on 2026-11-16. Rebuilt both independently into `/tmp`, using `gcc -O2 -march=native` and `-fopenmp` for p4par. All runs used `prlimit --as=12884901888`, `P4_REPS=2`, and the read-only `/home/exedev/paradigms/data/minio-p4` dataset.

Oracle results: p4par with `OMP_NUM_THREADS=1`, with `OMP_NUM_THREADS=8 OMP_THREAD_LIMIT=2`, and with `OMP_NUM_THREADS=8 P4_HUGE=0` each returned P4 OK: 60,733,909 bytes equal, 1,780,281 relocations equal, zero errors. The changed serial p4 also returned P4 OK. OpenMP runs used `OMP_WAIT_POLICY=passive`. Forced fallback succeeded even though MADV_HUGEPAGE failed on this VM; the diagnostic correctly said THP was not guaranteed.

The scan overwrites every active team's chunk total each repetition, uses the actual team size, barriers before and after the serial totals scan, and writes disjoint item offsets. Emit owns disjoint function ranges and vectors; concatenation is ordered by range index, hence preserves serial traversal order regardless of scheduling or a reduced OpenMP team. Sorting occurs after the repeat loop and is not charged to emit. Warmup initializes reusable vectors and output outside measured repetitions; emit includes concatenation. Function-range partition setup and allocations are outside timing and should be disclosed in the report.

One reporting caveat: the printed thread count is `omp_get_max_threads()`, so the constrained-team test prints “8 threads” despite actually running at most two. For the specified unconstrained measurements this is harmless, but reporting the actual initial team size would remove ambiguity. No blocking correctness findings on the supplied dataset. The no-patch diagnostic still traverses and decodes holes and collects relocations: its residual time is copy + traversal + relocations, not pure memcpy time; describe its difference as the incremental cost of integer/jump patching.
